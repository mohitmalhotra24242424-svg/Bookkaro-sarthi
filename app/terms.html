Terms

BookKro Sarthi use karne ka simple agreement.

Service kya hai

BookKro Sarthi ek AI conversational railway assistant hai — railway information dhone aur booking flow mein madad karta hai. Ye IRCTC ya Indian Railways ka official product nahi hai.

Information ki accuracy

Train, fare, availability aur PNR information verified railway data providers se aati hai. Agar provider data na de, hum honestly “unavailable” bolte hain — andaza nahi lagate. Travel se pehle official sources se verify karein.

Booking

Abhi booking demo flow hai — koi real ticket issue nahi hoti. Future mein real booking hogi toh explicit confirmation mandatory rahegi.

Zimmedari

Service “as is” milti hai. Provider downtime, quota limits ya galat information ke direct losses ki zimmedari BookKro Sarthi ki nahi hai.

Acceptable use

Assistant ko misuse na karein — spam, abuse ya automated scraping ke liye nahi.
